Things to watch out for

Russian examples are followed by their English spelling in brackets. Card spellings are approximate: Russian has sounds English can't spell exactly, and some letters change at the end of a word.

  1. False friends. В (V), Н (N), Р (R), С (S) and У (oo) look like English letters but sound different.
  2. Look-alikes by shape. Д (d) vs Л (l), И (ee) vs Н (n), П (p) vs Т (t), Б (b) vs В (v). Say each letter aloud when you flip the card.
  3. Е vs Э. Е is "ye", as in Елена (Yelena). Э is a plain "eh", as in Эльвира (Elvira).
  4. Four letters carry a "y" sound: Я (ya) as in Яна (Yana), Ю (yu) as in Юлия (Yulia), Е (ye) and Ё (yo). The dots on Ё are often dropped in everyday writing, so Алёна (Alyona) is often printed as Алена.
  5. Ш vs Щ. Ш is "sh", as in шоколад (shokolad). Щ is a longer, softer "shch", as in щенок (shchenok).
  6. New sounds. Ж (zh) as in Жанна (Zhanna), Х (kh, like Scottish "loch") as in хлеб (khleb), Ц (ts) as in цветок (tsvetok), Ч (ch) as in чай (chai).
  7. Ы is not И. Ы is a deep "ih" made further back in the mouth, as in сыр (syr). И is "ee", as in Ирина (Irina).
  8. Й is a glide, not a full vowel. It tucks onto the vowel before it, as in Майя (Maya) and чай (chai).
  9. Ь is the soft sign. It has no sound of its own and softens the consonant before it. In Любовь (Lyubov), the В (v) is soft.
  10. Ъ is the hard sign. It has no sound of its own and keeps sounds apart. In подъезд (podyezd), it separates Д (d) from Е (ye).
  11. Д and Б at the end of a word sound like "t" and "p". шоколад (shokolad) ends with a "t" sound, and хлеб (khleb) ends with a "p" sound.
  12. Some letters can't start a word: Ё, Й, Ъ, Ы and Ь. Those cards show the letter inside the word.
