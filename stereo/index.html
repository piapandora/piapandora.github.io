How to View Stereograms

Cross-View Mode (Default): Cross your eyes slightly so the two images overlap into a third 3D image in the middle.

Parallel View Mode: Relax your focus and look "through" the screen as if looking at a distant object until the two halves merge.

Press SPACEBAR anywhere to toggle viewing modes.

Press ENTER anywhere to download the result.
